FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking online means almost no waiting. Booked patients are seen first.

What is the consultation fee?

General consultation starts at ₹500. Specialist fees vary between ₹700 and ₹1,000. Follow-ups within 7 days are free.

Do you accept health insurance?

Yes, we accept most major insurance providers and offer cashless claims for eligible procedures.

Can I get my lab reports online?

Yes. Reports are sent to your WhatsApp and email, usually within 24 hours.

Feeling unwell? Don't wait.

Same-day appointments available. Our team is here to help.

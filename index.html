Do I really pay nothing for the preview?

Yes. I make a homepage preview with your business name for free. If you don't like it, you owe nothing.

Yes, 100%. The domain is registered in your name and you get all the code and logins.

Can I change things later myself?

With the Pro plan you get an admin panel to update text, prices and photos yourself. On other plans, small changes in the first 30 days are free.

What do I need to give you?

Your business name, logo (if you have one), phone number, address, services/prices and a few photos. I can write all the text for you.

What about hosting and maintenance?

Hosting for a simple business site is usually free or very cheap, and I set it all up. Optional maintenance is available monthly if you want me to handle updates.
